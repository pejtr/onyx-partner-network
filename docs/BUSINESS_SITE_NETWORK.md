# ONYX Business Site Network

ONYX Partner Network is not only a partner API surface. It is the commercial distribution edge for a portfolio of focused business sites that can share traffic, offers, attribution and monetization infrastructure without sharing private core systems.

## Operating principle

Each site must have a clear commercial job:

1. **Intent** — a specific audience with a specific problem or buying intent.
2. **Offer** — at least one monetization route that can convert.
3. **Distribution** — SEO, direct traffic, OMNI ADS STREAM or partner distribution.
4. **Attribution** — every outbound commercial path is measurable.
5. **Re-use** — content, data, audience or leads can create value for another network node.
6. **Fail-closed monetization** — no paid promotion or partner activation without a valid route and verified destination.

A site that has traffic but no commercial route is an audience asset.
A site that has an offer but no traffic is a conversion asset.
The network exists to connect the two.

## Site archetypes

### Lead marketplace
Examples: catering, local services, B2B requests.

**Revenue:** CPL, vendor subscription, premium placement, success fee.

### Affiliate intent site
Examples: travel, product discovery, comparison content.

**Revenue:** affiliate commission, CPC/CPA, direct partner placements.

### Digital product site
Examples: downloadable reports, tools, assessments, premium content.

**Revenue:** direct sale, subscription, upsell, affiliate back-end.

### SaaS utility
Examples: tools, calculators, workflow apps.

**Revenue:** subscription, modules, usage credits.

### Media / content vertical
Examples: recipes, wellbeing, senior lifestyle, tea culture.

**Revenue:** OMNI ADS STREAM, affiliates, lead generation, owned offers.

### Game / interactive property
Examples: browser games and branded experiences.

**Revenue:** sponsorship, advergames, in-game monetization, premium access, cross-promotion.

## Network loop

```text
Search / Social / Direct / Partner traffic
                  ↓
         Focused business site
                  ↓
      First-party intent signal
        ↙         ↓          ↘
   Direct sale   Lead     Affiliate route
        ↘         ↓          ↙
          Attribution + scoring
                  ↓
        ONYX Partner Network
                  ↓
  Cross-sell / OMNI ADS / partner routing
```

## Minimum launch gate

A new business site is only considered launch-ready when:

- the primary user intent is explicit above the fold;
- one primary conversion exists and works end-to-end;
- there is a valid monetization route;
- analytics/attribution is present;
- OMNI ADS STREAM slot is present where appropriate and remains fail-closed;
- privacy/legal copy matches the actual data collected;
- no fake testimonials, fake scarcity or unverifiable claims are used;
- mobile layout and core conversion path are tested;
- the site has a clear relationship to at least one other network node.

## Profitability score

Use a simple 100-point gate before spending money:

| Area | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Commercial intent | 20 |
| Margin / commission | 20 |
| Conversion friction | 15 |
| Traffic acquisition cost | 15 |
| Re-use across network | 10 |
| SEO / durable demand | 10 |
| Legal / operational friction | 10 |

**70+** = eligible for active distribution.  
**50–69** = organic validation first.  
**Below 50** = keep as experiment, do not fund with paid traffic.

Partner Network should be visible in:

- ONYXWEB as a growth/distribution capability;
- footer or partner pages of suitable network sites;
- partner onboarding documentation;
- public GitHub repository and issue templates;
- case studies that show **how multiple sites create value together**, not just isolated portfolio screenshots.
